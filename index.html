Sieve: Honest Photo Cleaner — privacy policy

Effective 19 October 2026. Applies to the Android app Sieve: Honest Photo Cleaner (com.kamistudio.sfog), by Kami Studio.

In short

  • The app collects nothing and sends nothing. It has no account, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting.
  • The release app does not have Android's Internet permission: it cannot connect to any server.
  • Your photos and videos are read, compared, compressed and moved to the trash on your phone only.

What the app keeps, on your phone

Your marks (Pick, Compress, Reject) and frame choicesSo a review survives closing the appThe app's private database

Nothing here leaves the phone through the app. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it. If Android's own backup is on, Android may include the app's data in your Google account's device backup; Kami Studio never receives it.

What you choose to send

Share and Edit in another app hand the photos you picked to the app you pick (Android's share sheet). That app's own privacy policy applies to what it receives. Sieve sends nothing itself.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.
